# Fantasy Weapons

A NeoForge mod for Minecraft 1.21.1 that adds 13 legendary RPG weapons. Each weapon levels up on its own, unlocks a
skill tree of abilities, and has custom poses, animations, VFX, HUD and sounds. All gameplay is server-authoritative.

The weapon models, textures and animations are the artist's original GeckoLib assets (with the artist's updated, higher
resolution textures) and are used unchanged. That
includes Infernochain's **Transform**, **Chainblade attack** and **Retraction** animations, and Eclipse Reaper's
**Transform**.

## Requirements

| | Version |
|---|---|
| Minecraft | 1.21.1 |
| NeoForge | 21.1.200 or newer (developed on 21.1.255) |
| GeckoLib | 4.9.3 (required dependency) |
| Java | 21 |

Build the jar with `./gradlew build`. It is written to `build/libs/fantasyweapons-<version>.jar`.

### Better Combat (optional)

The mod works with [Better Combat](https://modrinth.com/mod/better-combat) 2.4 or newer (with its own requirements,
playerAnimator and Cloth Config). Nothing changes without it. With it installed:

- Every blade swings in Better Combat's combos, chosen by weapon class: longswords slash, slash back and thrust;
  greatswords sweep, stab and sweep back hard; battle axes and scythes finish with a spin; Starforge sweeps and slams;
  Aetherlance thrusts twice and sweeps; Monolith sweeps both ways; Infernochain slashes and swipes. Combo finishers hit
  harder.
- Better Combat's hitboxes replace the weapons' own cleave, so every enemy in the swing is hit once. Damage, levels,
  crits, heavy attacks (sneak), on-hit effects, life steal, armor bonuses and hit effects all still come from this mod.
- Swing trails take the weapon's element colours. The weapons keep their own stances when idle.
- Staffs and spellbooks keep firing their bolt on attack. Legendary axes fight like Better Combat axes; legendary
  pickaxes and shovels stay digging tools, as Better Combat treats vanilla ones.
- While Soulreaper or Eclipse Reaper is thrown, Better Combat's swings are switched off until it comes back.

The combos live in `data/fantasyweapons/weapon_attributes/` (generated by `FWBetterCombat`), so a datapack can change them.
For development, `./gradlew runClient -PwithBetterCombat` runs the game with Better Combat installed.

### Punchy (optional)

The mod works with Punchy's first-person animations. The weapons, tools and other
items keep Punchy's animations. The Wayfarer's Codex, which is opened and read in the hands, would stay shut in
Punchy's hand, so whenever the game starts with both mods, the mod makes sure the Codex is on Punchy's item blacklist:
the Codex then lifts, opens and reads with its own animation. The entry shows up in Punchy's settings. To let Punchy
animate the Codex anyway, set `punchy_codex_own_animation = false` in `config/fantasyweapons-client.toml`, which takes
the entry off again.

Punchy draws the first-person arms and the held weapon itself, so the weapons' ability animations (raised while
charging, the swing on release, Monolith's plant, Cinder Cyclone, the form switch) are laid over its pose while they
play: the weapon moves in Punchy's hand and the arm reaches after it. Idle and attacks stay Punchy's (or Better
Combat's).

Punchy also dresses its first-person arms in the worn chestplate's sleeve, painted from the armor's vanilla texture. The
legendary armor is a 3D model with no such texture, so the arm came out black. When a legendary chestplate is worn,
the arm of its own model is drawn on Punchy's arm instead.

### Shader packs (Iris)

The effects are drawn with the mod's own shaders. While a shader pack is on, Iris draws nothing made with shaders it does
not know (unless its "allow unknown shaders" setting is on), so the effects draw with the game's own shaders instead,
which the pack replaces with its programs: glows as glowing eyes are, everything else (smoke, cracks, vines, chains, the
void) as translucent entities lit by the pack. The animated noise of energy and the swirl inside the void are left out
there. Nothing changes without a pack. For development, Iris, Sodium and a pack can be put in `run/mods` and
`run/shaderpacks`; the `shaderfx:<weapons>` screenshot script casts their abilities from the side to compare.

## Controls

All keys can be rebound under *Controls → Fantasy Weapons*.

| Key | Action |
|---|---|
| **R** (hold) | Charge the selected ability; release to cast. Releasing too early fizzles it. |
| **G** | Cycle the selected ability (sneak + G cycles backwards). |
| **F** | Switch form or mode (Infernochain sword ↔ chainblade, Eclipse Reaper light ↔ dark). |
| **K** | Open the weapon progression menu (skill tree, upgrades, upgrade-to-max, stats, model preview). |
| Attack | Melee with the weapon. Sneak-attack at full strength for a heavy attack. |

## Progression

- **Per-weapon EXP and levels (1–100).** Kills with the weapon (melee, abilities and damage over time) grant EXP,
  scaled by how strong the mob is.
- **Mastery points.** One per level, plus milestone bonuses. They unlock and upgrade abilities in the skill tree.
  Upgrades are validated on the server.
- **Damage scales with level and mastery.** Ability damage also scales with ability level and charge.
- **Custom status effects.** These don't use vanilla potions or particles, and appear on the HUD:
  - Void Mark, Solar Burn, Frostbite, Frozen, Berserker, Soul Drain, Nature Poison
  - Eclipse Light / Darkness, Gravity Bound, Inferno Overheat, Rooted, Staggered, Seared

## Configuration

Every setting can be changed in game: the gear button in the inventory's top right corner, above the crafting result,
opens the settings menu. *This game* holds the client settings (effects, HUD, notifications, compatibility); *this
world* holds the world's settings (progression, weapon EXP, damage, world destruction, and every ability's numbers,
weapon by weapon), shown once a world is open. Hover a setting for what it does, its default and its range; click a
switch or a choice to change it (right-click goes back), type a number and press Enter. Changes are saved at once. A
world's settings can be changed in single player; on a server they are shown but set by the server. The menu's Language page
lists the languages the mod is translated into and switches the game to the one chosen; the menu also opens the
game's Controls screen.

The same settings are in the config files:

- **`config/fantasyweapons-server.toml`**: EXP curve and sources, damage scaling, per-weapon base damage, and every
  ability parameter (damage, charge, cooldown, radius, …). The server syncs these values to clients.
- **`config/fantasyweapons-client.toml`**: effect quality, screen shake, effect limit, impact frames, and HUD layout and
  notifications.

### Effect quality

*Effect quality* (Visual effects) sets how much the effects draw: **Low-end PC**, Low, Medium, High (the default) or
Ultra. Every tier scales the effects' detail; the lower ones also show fewer effects at once and throw fewer blocks of
land through the air. At Low-end PC, Low and Medium the destruction of the great powers goes into the world a share
each tick instead of all in one frame, so a weak computer does not freeze when one lands (a block the game changes in
a chunk still waiting first lets that chunk's waiting land in, so nothing older covers it). Low-end PC is made for weak
computers: the fewest and simplest effects. The magic circles are drawn line by line rather than as stretched pictures,
so they are sharp at any size; the lower tiers draw their rings with fewer segments.

### Playing on a server

The destruction a power causes reaches each player over the network as fast as their connection takes it: every
rewritten chunk waits its turn (once, however often it is rewritten) and goes out with the newest blocks while the
connection keeps up, in packets well under the size a packet may be, so a power that rewrites thousands of chunks never
floods a player's connection. The `NetworkGameTests` write every packet the mod sends at its biggest and read it back
as a server would, checking nothing is left over and nothing is too big.

## Languages

English and Russian (*Русский*). The mod follows the game's language, which the Language page of the settings menu
switches between the languages the mod is translated into. Each piece of the mod's English text is its own
translation key, so a language file (`assets/fantasyweapons/lang/<code>.json`) maps the English to its translation, and
anything a file leaves out stays in English. Messages from the server are sent as translatable text, so on a server each
player reads them in their own language. The `russianTranslatesEveryText` GameTest gathers every text the mod's
definitions make (weapons, abilities, armor, materials, classes, quests, settings) and fails on any the Russian file
lacks; the `l10n` screenshot script films every screen and lists in `run/fw_untranslated.txt` whatever text it showed
that the current language has no translation for.

## Ground split API (Monolith)

Monolith's Earthshatter, Seismic Fissure, Tectonic Slam and Worldbreaker report every crack they open to
`com.fantasyweapons.api.GroundSplitAbility.execute(Context)`. The mod itself **never modifies terrain**. The default
handler does nothing, and a GameTest verifies that the arena blocks stay untouched.

You can plug in a ground-split system in either of two ways:

```java
// 1) install the implementation
GroundSplitAbility.setHandler(ctx -> MyGroundSplit.split(ctx.level(), ctx.origin(), ctx.direction(),
        ctx.length(), ctx.width(), ctx.depth()));

// 2) or listen to the cancellable event (fired before the handler)
NeoForge.EVENT_BUS.addListener((GroundSplitAbility.GroundSplitEvent e) -> {
    if (isProtected(e.context().level(), e.context().origin())) e.setCanceled(true);
});
```

`Context` carries:

- the player, level and weapon stack
- the crack's origin, horizontal direction, length, half-width and suggested depth
- the ability id and ability level

## Development

| Task | Command |
|---|---|
| Run the client | `./gradlew runClient` |
| Run all GameTests | `./gradlew runGameTestServer` |
| Scripted screenshot run | `./gradlew runClient -PfwDevtest=<script>` (screenshots land in `run/screenshots`) |
| Regenerate VFX textures, status icons and UI glyphs | `cd tools && python3 gen_textures.py` |
| Regenerate the fantasy UI art (frames, buttons, banners…) | `cd tools && python3 gen_ui.py` |
| Regenerate mesh materials (ice, thorn, petal, snowflake…) | `cd tools && python3 gen_materials.py` |
| Regenerate ability icons | `cd tools && python3 gen_icons.py [weapon …]` |

- **GameTests.** There are 67 tests. They cover the EXP curve, damage scaling, codecs, level-ups, server-side upgrade
  validation (including upgrade-to-max), fizzles, kill credit, the death dissolve and wall-safe teleports. They also cast every ability of every
  weapon against dummies, and check the ground-split hook and chainblade melee.
- **Screenshot scripts.** Available scripts:
  - `weapon:<id>` casts every ability of a weapon.
  - `ability:<weapon>/<ability>` captures one ability frame by frame.
  - `poses`, `plant`, `infernochain` and `menus` capture poses, Monolith's plant, Infernochain and the menus.
